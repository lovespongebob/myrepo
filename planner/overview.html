The Weekly Planner Overview

by Weiwei Hu

Copy last week’s tasks, remove the old projects, and update the progress. If you keep your weekly plan in a notebook, spreadsheet, or PPT, this probably sounds familiar. Then there’s the back-and-forth to collect updates and make sure everyone has the latest version.

I wanted to make this a little easier, so I created the Weekly Planner. Set it up once, then come back to the same place each week to plan work, update progress, and prepare your weekly report. Anyone you share it with can contribute whenever it works for them, and everyone can see the latest updates without needing to be there at the same time.

What can you do with it?

  • Create and update plans with anyone you share the planner with
  • Organize tasks by project and owner
  • See what’s not started, in progress, and doneNot startedIn progressDone
  • Sort tasks by due date, or leave the date open
  • Carry unfinished tasks into the next week
  • Generate weekly reports
  • Look back at previous weeks
  • Export and reopen your plan
