The Weekly Planner Overview

Copy last week’s tasks.Delete the old projects.Update the progress again.

When a weekly plan lives in a notebook, spreadsheet, or PPT, preparing the next update can mean repeating the same manual work. Sharing updates adds another step, especially when people are working at different times.

I wanted one shared place to plan work, update progress, and prepare the weekly report. Everyone can contribute on their own time, and updates appear in real time without waiting for me or anyone else to be there. That became the Weekly Planner: set it up once, use it each week, and carry unfinished tasks forward.

What can you do with it?

  • Organize tasks by project and owner
  • Share plans and update progress in real time
  • Track what’s not started, in progress, and doneNot startedIn progressDone
  • Carry unfinished tasks into the next week
  • Generate weekly reports
  • Keep a record of previous weeks
  • Export and reopen your plan
