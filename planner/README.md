# Weiwei’s Weekly Planner

A single-page weekly task planner built around one fixed entry form, so you stop copying last week's sheet, clearing it, and filling it in again.

## What it does

- **My Plan:** one form with project, task, status, due date, related item and notes, plus Fill, Save and Export buttons.
- **Carry over:** unfinished tasks from last week can be moved into this week in one click.
- **Weekly overview and report:** counts for Done, In progress and Not started update as you work. A text weekly report is generated for you to copy.
- **Sorting:** tasks sort by due date, and you choose whether tasks without a date go first or last.
- **Export and reopen:** export to JSON (full backup) or CSV, then reopen either file to restore tasks.
- **Three checks:** a built-in checklist to test sorting, counts, and export with example data before you rely on it.

## Files

- `app.html` is the page source. It is published as a claude.ai Artifact, where tasks are saved to the artifact's database.
- `index.html` is the standalone version generated by `build.sh`. Open it directly in a browser; tasks are saved in that browser's local storage, so export regularly to keep a copy.

Run `./build.sh` after editing `app.html` to regenerate `index.html`.

## Shared version: The Weekly Planner

`team.html` is a shared planner for two or more people, such as you and a partner. It is published as a separate Artifact and keeps the personal planner untouched.

- **Created by and Owner are separate:** both are names people type, and the planner never shows claude.ai account names. "Created by" is remembered on each device. "Owner" can be "Me" or anyone's name, even for people who never open the planner.
- **Several people per task:** type names in "Who", separated by commas (slashes work too), and the task counts for each person in their row, the "Mine" filter and the weekly summary.
- **Four statuses and big days:** Not started, In progress, Waiting and Done. Tick "Big day" for a birthday, recital, trip or special event; the next one shows as a countdown.
- **Home page with three tiles:** the page opens on a title and three tiles only: Overview (the one-pager), Plan Your Week (The Plan and the task list) and Weekly Summary (status, goals for the week, the task summary and the summary text). Clicking a tile opens that section, with "← All sections" and shortcuts to the other two at the top. Add `#intro`, `#planner`, `#summary` or `#home` to the link to open a view directly.
- **Weekly Summary tab:** status counts, "Goals for the week" (three fields you type in; click a goal's number to tick it off), and a Task summary table with who each task is for, its current status, highlights of the week and next step. The pencil on each row updates the week's highlight, the next step and the status in place. Goals also appear in the summary text and the Word, PowerPoint and PDF exports.
- **Highlights and key takeaways:** the Weekly Summary opens with "Highlights of the week", three lines each person fills in for themselves, next to Goals for the week, which uses the same three-field layout. Under the highlights, "Key takeaways" gives three short insights about the week, and under the goals, "What's next" lists the top three things to deal with next week. In the shared planner Claude writes both in one request from the week's highlights, goals and task status (the viewer is asked once to allow it, and Refresh asks again); elsewhere, or until Claude answers, they are worked out from the tasks. Copy summary copies the highlights, goals, takeaways and what's next.
- **Summary exports:** the Export menu and the Weekly Summary's "Download summary" save the current week's summary as Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF, following the category and person filters. The libraries (docx, PptxGenJS, jsPDF) load from jsDelivr only when a format is chosen.
- **Project timeline:** a second view that lists every task in a project across weeks, grouped by week, with "in N days" countdowns.
- **Form order:** Created by, Project, Task, Owner, Status, Due date, Related item, Notes.
- **Who filters:** Everyone, Owned by me, and Created by me.
- **By-person overview:** every owner gets a row with their own done, in-progress and not-started counts. Click a row to see only their tasks.
- **Your own progress note:** each person writes their own weekly note, so nobody overwrites anyone else.
- **Shared weekly summary:** the summary shows a combined total, then a section for each person with their note and tasks.

To plan together, share the artifact from its Share menu. Invite the people who plan with you as Contributors. With "Anyone with the link", other visitors see only the home tiles and the Overview; Plan Your Week and Weekly Summary stay locked for them. `team-index.html` is a local test copy (also generated by `build.sh`). Outside claude.ai it keeps Plan Your Week and Weekly Summary locked, like any visitor who is not an invited member; use `dist/weekly-planner.html` for a planner that works offline.

## Public sample and overview

- `sample.html` is a public sample of the shared planner, filled with fictional projects and tasks dated from the current week. Each visitor's changes stay in their own browser, and "Reset the sample" restores the starting data. `sample-index.html` is its offline copy.
- `overview.html` is a one-page overview by Weiwei Hu, with an "Explore the sample" button that opens the public sample.

## Shareable standalone files

`dist/` holds two single-file versions anyone can open in a browser, with no claude.ai account:

- `dist/weekly-planner.html` is the full planner. It saves to the browser it is opened in; Export keeps a copy or moves a plan to another device.
- `dist/weekly-planner-sample.html` is the same planner filled with example data, opening on the tiles page.

Word, PowerPoint and PDF exports load their libraries from jsDelivr, so they need an internet connection. Everything else works offline.
